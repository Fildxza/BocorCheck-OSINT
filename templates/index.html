Mitigation & Response Plan

Recommended Actions: Based on your breach exposure, follow these steps immediately to protect your accounts and personal data.
Immediate Actions (Urgent)
  • Change all passwords immediately
  • Enable 2FA/MFA wherever possible
  • Monitor bank & credit accounts
  • Check for unauthorized logins
  • Review active sessions
Short-term Actions (Week 1-2)
  • Place fraud alert with credit bureaus
  • Get free credit report
  • Update security questions
  • Review email recovery options
  • Update recovery phone/email
Long-term Actions (Ongoing)
  • Monitor credit reports regularly
  • Use unique passwords per account
  • Use password manager
  • Enable breach notifications
  • Regular security audits
